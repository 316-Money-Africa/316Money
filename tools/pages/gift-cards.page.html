What are digital gift cards?

Digital gift cards are prepaid cards from a brand that you buy online and give or use with that brand. They are delivered digitally, with no physical card.

Which gift cards can I purchase?

The gift cards you can buy depend on your location and on what each provider offers in your market. Browse the catalogue in the app to see what is available to you.

How do I purchase a gift card?

Choose a gift card in the 316 Money app, enter the amount you want, review the details and confirm your purchase.

How will I receive my gift card?

Your gift card is delivered digitally once your order is complete. Delivery details are shown in the app.

How can I check my order status?

You can follow your gift card orders and their status from your purchase history in the app.
