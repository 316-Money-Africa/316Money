Answers in one place

Explore with confidence before you apply

Explore common questions before applying to provide both funding and payout services.

{{slot:BothGuide/faqs ratio="2/1" class="ag-slot" alt="A question card about registering as both types of agent"}}
Can I register as both types of agent?

Yes. You can apply for both roles together. Each service is assessed separately and may carry its own requirements, so approval for one does not automatically enable the other.

How do the services differ?

A Payment Agent receives customer payments and funds eligible accounts (pay-ins). A Collection Agent pays out eligible withdrawals and remittance collections. The checks, rates and receipts differ for each.

Can I set different rates for each service?

Yes. Where authorised, rates are configured separately for each service and transaction direction, and are confirmed during approval.

Will I need different checks?

Business verification is shared, but each service has its own controls: payment confirmation for pay-ins, and recipient identification and payout authorisation for payouts.

How will customers find both services?

Your location can appear in the 316 Money app for either service, with nearby results shown for the service the customer is searching for.

Can I manage both in one workspace?

Yes. One Hotspots workspace covers both roles, with funding and payout activity, rates and reconciliation kept separate.
