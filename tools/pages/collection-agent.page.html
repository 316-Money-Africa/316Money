What is the difference between Collection and Payment Agents?

Collection Agents take in cash and help customers fund their accounts or receive eligible payouts. Payment Agents pay out cash against eligible requests. A Hotspot can offer one or both, depending on the services it is approved for.

Can my business offer both services?

Yes. Eligible Hotspots can offer collection and payment services together, so one location can serve more customer needs.

Can I set my own rates?

You can configure the rates for eligible local and international transactions within the rules and limits that apply to your services, and request publication of your changes.

How do customers find my Hotspot?

Customers discover Hotspots in the 316 Money app based on proximity, with the closest relevant agents shown first, along with availability, ratings and reviews.

Do agents get web and mobile tools?

Yes. Agents work from a dedicated web application for a complete view of operations and use the mobile app to serve customers and manage requests on the move.

How are agent tiers determined?

Tiers reflect the services a Hotspot is approved for and its activity. Your onboarding steps and verification results determine where you start.
