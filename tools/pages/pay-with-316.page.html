What does Pay with 316 Money mean?

It means paying participating merchants from your 316 Money balance at supported checkouts.

Where can I use 316 Money to pay?

You can use it at participating merchants that support 316 Money at checkout. Availability depends on the merchant and on supported payment arrangements.

How do I pay with 316 Money?

At a supported checkout, choose 316 Money as your payment option, review the merchant and amount, and authorise the payment.

Do I need money in my 316 Money balance?

Payments are made from your available 316 Money balance, so you need enough available balance to cover the amount.

How can I confirm whether my payment went through?

You can check the status of each payment in your transaction history in the app.

What happens if my payment fails?

If a payment does not complete, the app shows its status. Check your transaction history for the outcome and contact support if you need help.

Can I request a refund?

Refunds depend on the merchant’s policy and on the payment. Contact the merchant first, and contact 316 Money support if you need further help.
