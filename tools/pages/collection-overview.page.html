What is a collection agent?

Offer more services. Give customers more reasons to visit

Collection Agents are the pay-out side of the 316 Hotspots network. They act as local cash-access points, helping approved customers receive physical cash against eligible wallet withdrawals or remittance payout requests.

  • Serve 316 Pay businesses

    Support eligible business cash-access requests linked to 316 Pay.

  • Local & international remittances

    Provide a convenient collection point for beneficiaries receiving funds through supported domestic and cross-border remittance services.

What does a Collection Agent do?

A Collection Agent pays out cash to customers against eligible wallet withdrawals, business payouts and supported remittances, after checking the request and the recipient. Every transaction is verified and recorded.

Can I set my own collection and payout rates?

You can configure applicable collection rates for eligible local and international transactions within the rules and limits that apply to your tier, and submit changes for approval before they are published.

How do customers find my location?

Customers discover participating Collection Agents in the 316 Money app, with the closest locations shown first, alongside your services, rates and customer reviews.

Will I get both a web application and a mobile app?

Yes. A dedicated web application gives you a complete view of your operations, and the mobile app lets you serve customers and manage requests on the move.

Can I also become a Payment Agent?

Yes. Eligible Hotspots can offer collection and payment services together once approved for both, so one location can serve more customer needs.

How are payout limits and cash requirements determined?

Limits and cash requirements depend on the agent tier your business is approved for, your location and the services you offer. They are confirmed during onboarding and review.
