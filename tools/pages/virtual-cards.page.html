What is a virtual card?

A virtual card is a digital card linked to your 316 Money account. You can use its details for online payments, and manage it from the app.

Where can I use a virtual card?

You can use a virtual card for online checkout with merchants that accept card payments. Acceptance depends on the merchant and your market.

How do I manage my card?

Where available for your card, you can lock it, review security controls and update settings from the 316 Money app.

Is a virtual card available in my country?

Availability depends on your location and account eligibility. The app shows the card options available to you.
