What are third-party integrations?

Third-party integrations connect 316 with the platforms and applications you already use, so payments and business records stay in step.

Which third-party platforms can I connect to 316 Business?

Supported platforms cover online stores, accounting and automation tools. The list grows as new connections are added.

Can I connect 316 Business to my accounting software?

Yes, where your accounting software is supported. Payments and records can then be kept in sync.

Can I automate tasks using integrations?

Yes. Connected tools can pass supported information between systems, so routine tasks run without manual work.

What are APIs, and how do they work with 316 Business?

An API lets your own software talk to 316. Developers can use it to build custom payment workflows and react to events through webhooks.
