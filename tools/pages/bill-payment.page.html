What bills can I pay with 316 Money?

Supported categories include electricity, TV and entertainment, internet and data, and other services. What you can pay depends on your location and on provider availability.

How do I pay a bill?

Choose a service in the 316 Money app, enter the required account, meter or customer reference details, review the amount and charges, then confirm.

Are all bill payment services available in every country?

No. Bill payment services vary by location and provider. The app shows the services available to you.

How can I check my payment status?

You can follow the status of each bill payment in your transaction history in the app.

What happens if my payment is pending?

A pending payment is still being processed. Its status is shown in your transaction history and updates when it completes.

What happens if a payment fails?

If a payment does not complete, the app shows its status. Check your transaction history for the outcome and contact support if you need help.

Are there additional charges?

Any applicable charges are shown before you confirm your payment, so you can review them first.

Can I pay school fees through 316 Money?

School fee payments are available where supported. Check the options for your market in the app.
