How do I get started with 316?

Download the 316 Money app, create an account with your email and phone number, and complete the identity checks. Once you are verified you can add money, send payments and use the features available in your country.

How can I get help with a payment?

Use the form above and choose “Help with a payment”. Include the amount, the date and the reference shown in the app, and our team will look into it and get back to you.

Which countries can use 316 Money?

316 Money is switched on country by country as our partners and approvals allow. The countries and features available to you are shown when you create your account.

How do I contact 316 Business?

Choose “316 Business” in the enquiry list above and tell us about your business. Our team will reply with the next steps for accepting payments, invoicing and the other business tools.

How can I become a Hotspot?

Choose “Becoming a Hotspot agent” above, or start your application from the Hotspots pages. We assess each application, including verification and location checks, before services are enabled.

How do I report a failed transaction?

Choose “A failed transaction” in the enquiry list and share the reference, the amount and the date. We will check the status and tell you what happens next.
