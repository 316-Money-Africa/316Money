What are store gift cards?

A store gift card is a digital card with a value that a customer can buy for someone else. The recipient uses it to pay for eligible purchases with your business.

How can my business offer gift cards?

Set up your gift card from your dashboard, choose how it looks and the amounts customers can buy, then share your store link or add it to your website.

Can I customise my gift cards with my brand?

Yes. Add your logo, choose the card colour and set the denominations so every gift card looks like your business.

Can customers purchase gift cards for other people?

Yes. Customers can buy a gift card and share it with a friend or family member by message or link.

How do recipients redeem gift cards?

Recipients enter the gift card code at checkout, or show it in store, and the value is applied to their purchase.

Can gift cards be used for multiple purchases?

Yes. A gift card keeps its remaining balance after each purchase until the full value has been used.
