Which payment methods can I accept?

Where available in your market: mobile money, cards, bank transfer, USSD, 316 wallet balance, and gift cards and vouchers.

Is there a transaction fee?

Any applicable fees are shown to you before you confirm a transaction. Check the fee details in your dashboard for your market.

How fast do I receive payments?

Settlement timing depends on the payment method and your market. Paid and pending payments are visible in your dashboard as they happen.

How do I track my transactions?

Every payment appears in your dashboard with its status, amount and reference, so you can review details and reconcile with ease.

Can I integrate 316 with my website?

Yes. Use hosted checkout and payment links with no code, or integrate through the API and signed webhooks.
