What card options are available?

316 Money offers physical and virtual card options. Which ones you can use depends on your location and eligibility.

Card availability varies by country and region. Check the options for your market in the app before getting started.

Can I manage my card in the app?

Yes. Where card controls are available for your card, you can manage them from the 316 Money app.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Requirements depend on the card and your market, and may include account and verification checks. The app shows what applies to you.
