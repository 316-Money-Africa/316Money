What’s the difference between virtual cards and gift cards?

A virtual card is a digital card linked to your 316 Money account that you can use for online payments. A gift card is a prepaid digital card from a brand, bought to give or to spend with that brand.

How do virtual cards work?

Where available for your account, you can create a virtual card in the app, use its details for online payments, and manage it with the card controls shown in the app.

How can I explore available gift cards?

Browse the gift card catalogue in the 316 Money app. The brands shown depend on your location and provider availability.

Are virtual cards available in every country?

No. Virtual card availability depends on your location and account eligibility. The app shows the options available to you.

Are all gift cards available in my location?

Not always. The gift cards you can see depend on your location and on what each provider makes available there.
