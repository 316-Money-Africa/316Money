users worldwide

How can my organisation start accepting donations?

Set up your donation page from your business dashboard, choose how it looks and the amounts supporters can give, then share the link or add it to your website.

Can donors choose their contribution amounts?

Yes. You can offer suggested amounts and let supporters enter their own, so they give what works for them.

Can I share my donation page?

Yes. Every donation page has its own link that you can share by message, email or social media.

Can I track donations?

Yes. Your dashboard shows each contribution and the total received, so you always know how your campaign is doing.

Can supporters make recurring donations?

Supporters can give once, and recurring giving can be offered so they can contribute on a schedule that suits them.

Can I track digital card sales and redemptions?

Yes. You can see digital card sales, purchases and redemption activity from one place in your dashboard.
