How can I top up airtime with 316 Money?

Open the 316 Money app, choose airtime top-up, select a supported network, enter the number and amount, then review the details and confirm.

Which mobile networks are supported?

Supported networks depend on your location and on provider availability. The app shows the networks available to you.

Can I buy airtime for someone else?

Yes, where supported. Enter the recipient’s mobile number and network when you top up.

What happens if my airtime top-up is delayed?

Some top-ups can take a little longer to arrive. You can follow the status of your payment in your transaction history in the app.

What happens if my top-up fails?

If a top-up does not complete, the status is shown in the app. Check your transaction history for the outcome and contact support if you need help.

Are there additional charges?

Any applicable charges are shown before you confirm your purchase, so you can review them first.
