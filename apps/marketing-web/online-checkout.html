users worldwide

What payment methods are supported?

Checkout supports the payment methods available in your market, such as cards, mobile money and bank options, so customers can pay the way they prefer.

Is the checkout secure?

Yes. Card details are entered on a secure checkout and payments are processed securely, so your customers can pay with confidence.

Can I customise the checkout page?

Yes. You can add your logo and brand name and choose the colours so the checkout matches your business.

Does checkout work on mobile devices?

Yes. The checkout adapts to phones, tablets and computers, so paying is just as easy on every screen.

How do I integrate checkout into my website?

You can connect checkout through supported platforms and plug-ins, or use our APIs and documentation to build it into your own site.
