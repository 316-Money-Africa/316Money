FAQs

Find answers about Payment Agent eligibility, account-funding services, rates and day-to-day operations.

Answers in one place

Explore with confidence before you apply

Explore common questions before applying to accept eligible customer payments.

What does a payment agent do?

A Payment Agent receives an eligible payment from a customer and completes an account-funding request, so money moves from your location into the customer’s supported digital account. Each transaction is verified and recorded.

Can I choose my payment service?

You can offer the services you are approved for, such as 316 Money wallet funding, business account funding or supported international funding. Availability depends on your location and tier.

How can payment agents earn?

Agents can earn from completed eligible funding transactions under their approved pricing and service terms. Rates and limits are confirmed during approval.

What should I verify before accepting a payment?

Confirm the destination account, funding amount, accepted method and applicable charges, and follow the required checks before you start the funding workflow.

How will customers find my business?

Customers discover nearby Payment Agents in the 316 Money app, with the closest locations shown first alongside your services, availability and reviews.

When can I begin?

You can begin once your application, verification and setup are complete and your services have been enabled for your location.
