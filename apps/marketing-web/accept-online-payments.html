What is 316 Business?

316 Business is the set of tools that lets your business accept online payments, send payouts and manage settlements from one 316 Pay account.

How do I create a 316 Business account?

Sign up with your email and phone number, complete the required business verification, and you can start with test keys straight away.

What payment methods can my business accept?

Where available in your market: mobile money, cards, bank transfer, USSD, 316 wallet balance, and gift cards and vouchers.

What fees does 316 Business charge?

Any applicable fees are shown to you before you confirm a transaction. Check the fee details in your dashboard for your market.

How do payouts and settlements work?

You can pay suppliers, staff and partners individually or in bulk, with settlements going to your approved account.

Can I integrate 316 Business with my website or application?

Yes. Use hosted checkout and payment links with no code, or integrate through the API and signed webhooks.
