What can I do with 316 Money?

Hold money in multiple currencies, send and receive payments, pay bills, buy airtime and data, and create virtual cards. Which features you see depends on your country.

How do I get started with 316 Money?

Download the app from the App Store or Google Play, create an account with your email and phone number, and complete identity verification.

Can I send and receive money with 316 Money?

Yes. You can send and receive money with other 316 Money users and to supported destinations, with the fees and exchange rate shown before you confirm.

Can I hold money in different currencies?

Yes. Supported currencies are available as wallets in the app. Availability depends on your country.

Can I save money towards specific goals?

Yes. Create a named savings pot with a target amount and a timeframe, and add money to it whenever you like.

Are all 316 Money features available in every country?

No. Features are switched on country by country as our partners and approvals allow.
