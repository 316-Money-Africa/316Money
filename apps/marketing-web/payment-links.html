Questions you might want to ask

more than 10K+
users worldwide

Can I customise my payment link?

Yes. Add your brand logo, colors and details so every link looks like your business.

Do payment links expire?

A link stays active until you switch it off. You can also set an optional expiry date.

Will I get notified when someone pays?

Yes. You get an instant notification in your dashboard and by email as soon as a payment is made.

Can I use one payment link multiple times?

Yes. Share one link with many customers and use it again and again, or limit it to a single payment.

How do I track payments made through my links?

Every payment appears in your dashboard with its status, amount and reference, so you can review details and reconcile with ease.
