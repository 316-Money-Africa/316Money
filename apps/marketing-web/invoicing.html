How do I create an invoice?

Add your customer, the service or product and the payment details, then review and customise your invoice before you send it.

Can I customise my invoices?

Yes. Add your logo, brand name and colors so every invoice looks like your business.

How can I send invoices to customers?

Share a secure invoice with your customer by email or as a link.

Can customers pay invoices online?

Yes. Customers can open the invoice and pay online through a simple checkout.

How do I track outstanding invoices?

Every invoice shows its payment status in your dashboard, so you can see what is paid, pending or overdue and know the moment you get paid.
