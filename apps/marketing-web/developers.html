Questions you might want to ask

more than 10K+
users worldwide

How do I get API access?

Create a 316 business account, then generate your API keys from the developer section of your dashboard.

Where can I find the API documentation?

The API documentation, quickstart guides and code examples are available from the developer resources on this page.

Does 316 provide webhooks?

Yes. Webhooks notify your system when events such as a successful payment happen, so you can automate your workflows.

Can I test payments before going live?

Yes. Use your test keys to try your integration with test payments, then switch to your live keys when you are ready.

How do I get technical support?

Contact our team from your dashboard or through Contact sales and we will help you with your integration.
