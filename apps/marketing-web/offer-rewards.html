What is the 316 Business rewards feature?

Rewards let your business give customers points or benefits for shopping with you, so they have more reasons to come back.

How can my business reward customers?

Set up a rewards programme from your dashboard, choose what earns rewards, and customers collect them automatically as they pay.

Can I customise my rewards programme?

Yes. You choose how customers earn rewards and which benefits they can unlock, so the programme fits your business.

When does a business receive payment?

Payments are settled to your account on your normal settlement schedule. Rewards do not delay what you are paid for a sale.

How do customers earn rewards?

Customers earn rewards when they make eligible purchases, for example points for every payment they complete with your business.

Can customers redeem their rewards?

Yes. Customers can use the rewards they have earned on eligible purchases, and you can see every redemption in your dashboard.
