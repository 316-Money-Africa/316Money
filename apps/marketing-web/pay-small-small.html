What is Pay Small Small?

Pay Small Small lets your customers split a larger purchase into smaller instalments, so they can buy now and pay over time while you are paid on schedule.

How do installment payments work?

The customer chooses a plan at checkout and pays the first instalment straight away. The remaining payments follow the schedule they agreed to.

Can customers choose their preferred payment plan?

Yes. You offer the available plans, such as pay in 3 or pay in 4, and the customer picks the one that fits their budget.

When does a business receive payment?

You receive the first instalment at checkout. Each later instalment is settled to you as it is collected, and every payment shows in your workspace.

Who is responsible for collecting subsequent installments?

Collection follows the agreed schedule automatically, and customers are reminded before each instalment is due, so you do not have to chase payments.

Are there additional fees for installment payments?

Any fees are shown before a customer confirms their plan, so the cost of paying in instalments is clear from the start.

What happens when a customer misses a payment?

The customer is reminded and given the chance to complete the payment. You can see the status of every plan, including overdue instalments, in one place.
