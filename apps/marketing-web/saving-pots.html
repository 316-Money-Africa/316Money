What are saving pots?

Saving pots let you set money aside for a specific goal, such as rent, a purchase or a trip, and track how close you are to it.

What can I save for?

Anything you like: rent and bills, a new purchase, travel plans or any goal you want to keep visible.

Can I create more than one saving pot?

Yes. You can create a separate pot for each goal.

Can I set a target for my saving pot?

Yes. Set how much you want to save and watch your progress toward it.

Can I withdraw money from a saving pot?

Yes, under the terms of the saving pot. Check the pot details in the app before withdrawing.

Can I automate my savings?

Where available, you can set up recurring contributions so your pot grows without you having to remember.
