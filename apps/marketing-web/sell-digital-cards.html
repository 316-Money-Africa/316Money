What are digital cards?

Digital cards are cards with a stored value, such as gift and entertainment cards, that customers can buy and use online. You can resell them through 316 Pay or give them as rewards.

What types of digital cards can I sell?

You can choose from the digital card brands available on 316 Pay, across shopping, entertainment, gaming and crypto, and pick the ones that suit your customers.

Can I customise my digital cards?

You can choose which card brands to offer and configure their denominations, so customers see the options that work for your business.

How do customers purchase digital cards?

Customers pick a card, choose a denomination and pay at checkout. The card is delivered to them digitally as soon as the payment succeeds.

How do customers redeem their cards?

Customers use the card code with the brand it was issued for, following that brand’s redemption steps, or you can give them as rewards for them to redeem.

Can I track digital card sales and redemptions?

Yes. Your dashboard shows digital card sales, purchases and redemption activity in one place, so you always know how your cards are performing.
