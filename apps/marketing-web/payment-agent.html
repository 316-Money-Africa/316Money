What is payment agents?

Make cash deposits easier. Grow your business

Payment Agents form the pay-in side of the 316 Hotspots network. They facilitate eligible customer deposits and account funding transactions, helping money move from local payment points into supported digital accounts.

  • 316 Money account funding

    Help individuals add funds to their eligible 316 Money wallets through an approved in-person funding process, with transaction confirmation and records.

  • Business account funding

    Support eligible businesses that need to fund their 316 Money or supported 316 Pay balances through approved local payment channels.

  • Local & international payments

    Facilitate eligible payment and funding transactions linked to supported domestic and cross-border services, subject to market rules and partner availability.

What does a 316 Hotspots Payment Agent do?

A Payment Agent receives cash or an approved payment from a customer and completes an eligible funding or payment request, so money moves from your location into the customer’s supported digital account. Every transaction is verified and recorded.

Can a small kiosk become a Payment Agent?

Yes. Roadside kiosks, shops, agency operators and banking partners can all take part, each within the transaction capacity, limits and controls approved for their business.

Can I set my own rates?

You can configure applicable pay-in rates for eligible local and international transactions within the rules and limits that apply to your tier, and submit changes for approval before they are published.

How will customers find my business?

Customers discover nearby Payment Agents in the 316 Money app, with the closest relevant locations shown first, alongside your services, rates and customer reviews.

Will I have both web and mobile access?

Yes. A dedicated web application gives you a complete view of your operations, and the mobile app lets you serve customers and manage requests on the move.

Can I become both a Payment and Collection Agent?

Yes. Eligible Hotspots can offer payment and collection services together once approved for both, so one location can serve more customer needs.
