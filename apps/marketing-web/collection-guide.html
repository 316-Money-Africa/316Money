FAQs

Answers in one place

Explore with confidence before you apply

Explore common questions before applying to provide eligible customer payouts.

What does a collection agent do?

A Collection Agent pays out eligible funds to customers, such as account withdrawals and authorised remittance collections, so money moves from your location to the right person. Each payout is verified and recorded.

Can I choose my collection service rates?

You can set permitted withdrawal, payout or remittance-collection rates where your location supports it. Rates and limits are confirmed during approval.

How can collection agents earn?

Agents can earn from completed eligible payout and collection transactions under their approved pricing and service terms.

What must I check before a payout?

Verify the customer and the payout authorisation, confirm the amount, recipient details and applicable charges, and follow the required checks before releasing funds.

How will customers find my business?

Customers discover nearby Collection Agents in the 316 Money app, with the closest locations shown first alongside your services, availability and reviews.

When can I begin?

You can begin once your application, verification and setup are complete and your services have been enabled for your location.
