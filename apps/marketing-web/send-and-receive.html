Can I receive money from the UK?

Yes, where UK-origin transfers are enabled for your account. The money is credited to your wallet once the transfer has been matched and settled.

Which countries are supported?

Supported countries and transfer routes are switched on one by one. The app shows what is available for your country.

How do I track a transfer?

Open the transfer in the app to see its status, reference and expected delivery information.

What fees apply?

Fees and the exchange rate are shown before you confirm any transfer.

What if a transfer is delayed?

Some transfers take longer while they are checked or processed by our partners. If yours is taking longer than expected, contact support with your transfer reference.
