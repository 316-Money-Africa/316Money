# audit-logger

Append-only, tamper-evident business and security audit trail.

**SRS:** SRS-316-AFR-001 §4, §16

## Requirement IDs

- `AFR-CORE-004` — All money movements SHALL have audit-linked states and recoverable error handling.
- `SEC-008` — All high-risk security and financial events SHALL produce tamper-evident audit records.
- `LINK-009` — All workspace switches SHALL generate security audit events and invalidate inappropriate cached data.
- `ADM-006` — The system SHALL record who changed configurations, previous/new values, justification and effective date.
- `NFR-12` — Audit: Immutable business event history with approved retention policy
