# approvals

Maker-checker approval workflow for privileged finance, rate, payout, config and compliance actions.

**SRS:** SRS-316-AFR-001 §9, §16

## Requirement IDs

- `LINK-006` — High-risk actions including payout, rate changes, operator invitations and account changes SHALL require step-up verification and/or maker-checker approval.
- `SEC-007` — Privileged finance, rate, payout and compliance overrides SHALL use maker-checker approval where risk warrants.
- `HOT-REG-006` — Material changes to agent rates, bank destinations or operator permissions SHALL be auditable and may require approval.
- `AFR-CORE-006` — Country configurations SHALL be versioned and require an authorised approval workflow for financial-impacting changes.
