# ratings-discovery

Hotspot location search, availability, ratings and moderation, and ranking.

**SRS:** SRS-316-AFR-001 §8.3

## Requirement IDs

- `MON-EXT-005` — Users SHALL locate approved Hotspots by geography, availability, service type, displayed charges and applicable ratings.
- `HOT-OPS-005` — Ratings SHALL only be accepted from eligible completed transactions and SHALL support moderation and appeals.
- `HOT-OPS-006` — Agent ranking SHALL use documented scoring factors and SHALL not imply regulated status or financial safety.
