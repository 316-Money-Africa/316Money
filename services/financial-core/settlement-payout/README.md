# settlement-payout

Merchant and agent settlements and payout scheduling.

**SRS:** SRS-316-AFR-001 §4, §7

## Requirement IDs

- `PAY-011` — Settlement reports SHALL itemise gross collections, provider fees, platform fees, refunds, reserves and net payouts.
- `HOT-OPS-003` — Agent earnings SHALL itemise gross fees, platform commissions, adjustments and settlement status.
- `ADM-007` — Dashboards SHALL distinguish actual settled revenue from pending gross payment volume.
