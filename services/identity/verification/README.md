# verification

KYC/KYB, document checks and sanctions/PEP screening (calls provider-adapters/kyc-providers).

**SRS:** SRS-316-AFR-001 §4, §16

## Requirement IDs

- `MON-001` — Mobile onboarding SHALL collect required country, residency, contact details, consent and verification evidence.
- `HOT-REG-003` — Agent activation SHALL require KYC/KYB, risk screening, agreement acceptance, compliance approval and permitted service assignment.
- `SEC-005` — Compliance controls SHALL support KYC/KYB, sanctions/PEP screening, AML monitoring and case management.
