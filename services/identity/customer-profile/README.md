# customer-profile

Consumer personal data, preferences and lifecycle (restriction, dormancy, closure).

**SRS:** SRS-316-AFR-001 §4, §6.1

## Requirement IDs

- `MON-001` — Mobile onboarding SHALL collect required country, residency, contact details, consent and verification evidence.
- `MON-007` — Account restriction, dormancy, closure and residual funds workflows SHALL be supported.
- `SEC-004` — PII SHALL be minimised, access logged, retention controlled and subject-rights workflows supported.
